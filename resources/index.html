Live weather and Earth views

earth · code
An animated globe of wind, ocean currents and air quality from forecast data, by Cameron Beccario. The code shows how it's made.
Fluid Earth
Another animated globe of Earth data.
Ventusky
Forecast maps of wind, rain, temperature and more.
Windy
Forecast maps that let you compare different weather models.
Zoom Earth
Near-live satellite imagery, storms and fires.
NASA Eyes on the Earth
NASA's Earth-observing satellites and what they measure, in 3D.
Climate Reanalyzer
Charts and maps of climate and forecast data, from the Climate Change Institute at the University of Maine.
NVIDIA Earth-2
NVIDIA's platform for AI weather and climate models, a corporate vision of a digital twin of the Earth.

Data and imagery

Open-Meteo
Free weather data, forecasts and history back to 1940. No account needed.
Copernicus Data Space
Europe's satellite imagery and data, including the Sentinel satellites.
ACLED
Data on political violence and protest around the world.

Climate and policy simulators

En-ROADS
Change climate policies and see the effect on warming, by Climate Interactive and MIT Sloan.
Half-Earth Socialism
A game about planning the world's economy within planetary limits.

Mapping and visualization tools

kepler.gl
Drop in a data file and map it in the browser, no code needed.
deck.gl
A code library for large data maps on the web.
CesiumJS
A code library for 3D globes and terrain.
Re:Earth
Open-source tools for making 3D maps on the web.
F4map
A 3D city map built from OpenStreetMap.
Ancient Earth
Where your city was on the globe hundreds of millions of years ago.

Simulation, digital twins and cities

ArchiDynamics
Software for architects that simulates wind, sun, daylight and climate around buildings.
A/B Street
A traffic simulation game: change a city's streets and see what happens.
UrbanSim
Software used to model how cities grow, with case studies.
CityScope
MIT Media Lab's tools for planning cities with physical and digital models together.
PLATEAU
Japan's open 3D models of its cities, from the government.
NetLogo Web
Build simple models of many agents, such as people, animals or particles, in the browser.

Geopolitics and war simulation

NUKEMAP
The effects of a nuclear explosion on any place, by the historian Alex Wellerstein.
Nuclear War Simulator
A simulation of nuclear war focused on its human consequences.
Liveuamap
A live map of conflicts, built from news and social media.

Investigation and open-source research

Forensic Architecture
A research group that uses architecture and spatial analysis to investigate violence.
Cloud Studies
Forensic Architecture's work on toxic clouds, tear gas and air as a site of violence.
TimeMap · Mariupol
Forensic Architecture's open tool for mapping events in time and space, and a project built on it about Mariupol.
Bellingcat toolkit
Tools for open-source investigation, from satellite imagery to flight tracking.
Conflict Urbanism: Aleppo
A layered map of damage in Aleppo, by the Center for Spatial Research at Columbia University.

Artists and workshops

cloud translations
Everest Pipkin's 2015 project that read clouds, wires and trees in Street View as poetry.
Geobodies
Lucia Rebolino's AA Visiting School workshop in Venice, 2024: satellite indexes of water and vegetation with Google Earth Engine.
