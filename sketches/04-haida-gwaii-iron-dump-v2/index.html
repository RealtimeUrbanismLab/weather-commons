Haida Gwaii Iron Dump

July to August 2012. The Haida Salmon Restoration Corporation, paid for by the village of Old Massett, put about 120 t (132 US tons) of iron sulphate and iron oxide into an eddy 230 mi west of Haida Gwaii. The green on the sea is chlorophyll and the streaks are currents, both as satellites measured them. The stems are a simulation.

Sources and limits

  1. Chlorophyll: daily chlorophyll-a readings by MODIS on Aqua and Terra and VIIRS on Suomi NPP (NASA GIBS, public domain, retrieved 9 November 2026). For each of 19 dates the middle value of every valid reading within 8 days is kept. Observed, gathered over about two weeks; satellites see only the top few yards of water. Drawn from about 0.3 to 4 mg/m³. The relief on the sea shows the same value.
  2. Currents: derived for this sketch from JPL MEaSUREs gridded sea-surface height anomalies (satellite altimetry, NASA GIBS, public domain, retrieved 9 November 2026), one field every 5 days. Water circles a rise or a dip in the sea surface, so the slope of the surface gives the current. Streaks show direction and relative speed, sped up. Features smaller than about 60 mi are smoothed away.
  3. The picture: MODIS Terra and Aqua true colour (NASA GIBS, public domain, retrieved 9 November 2026). Sea: every picture within 8 days of each date, the third-darkest view of each pixel, with remaining cloud and haze pressed down to dark water. Land: one sharp composite, 21 August to 6 September 2012. Clouds are left out on purpose.
  4. Land relief: AWS Terrain Tiles (Tilezen terrarium, zoom 9), retrieved 9 November 2026. Heights are exaggerated; the shading is computed, lit from the north-west.
  5. Coast, borders and the region under the block: Natural Earth 1:50m, public domain. The coastline inside the block is from an earlier source still to be recorded.
  6. The plankton stems and the iron are a toy model carried by the measured currents: synthetic. A stem's height shows how dense the simulated plankton are. The ship's track and dates are approximate.
