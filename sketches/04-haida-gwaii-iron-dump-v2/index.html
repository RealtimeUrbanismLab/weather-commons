Haida Gwaii Iron Dump

July–August 2012. The Haida Salmon Restoration Corporation put about 120 t (132 US tons) of iron sulphate into an eddy 190 mi west of Haida Gwaii. The sea below is simulated; the picture over it is stitched from the nineteen MODIS Aqua passes that best witness each part of it. Turn it, scrub it, rest on a patch to read it.

Sources and limits

  1. NASA MODIS Aqua true colour and chlorophyll via Worldview, public domain. 19 passes, 14 June – 28 October 2012, 49–58° N, 145–127° W. Observed; cloud hides the water on most days.
  2. Coastline and graticule: cartographic. Coastline source to be recorded.
  3. Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (Tilezen terrarium, zoom 7; SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1 and others), retrieved 5 November 2026, resampled to 360 × 180. Land only; height exaggerated.
  4. The sea is a toy: particles, three eddies, grazing. Synthetic, not a forecast or a hindcast. The ship's route is approximate. The reported bloom was about 10,000 km² (3,900 sq mi).
