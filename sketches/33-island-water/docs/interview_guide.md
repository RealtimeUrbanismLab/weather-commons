# Interview guide

Consent first: to record, to quote, to name. Notes go in `private/`, never in the
public sketch folder. Offer to share the result.

## Municipality / water utility (Sifnos)

1. Which sources supply which villages, in winter and in summer?
2. Capacities and age of the desal units; energy use; breakdowns since 2024.
3. Reservoir volume and fill over recent years; borehole levels and salinity.
4. How were residents informed of the 2024 emergency and the 2026 measures?
5. What will the new 1,000 m³/day unit change? What is planned beyond it?

## Poulati landowners and farmers

6. How are "waters" scheduled now? What has changed since 2022?
7. What happens in a dry year? Who decides?
8. What would help keep the system alive?

## Residents (older and younger)

9. How did your household manage water before desalination? Do you still use a cistern?
10. What did you notice in the summers of 2024 and 2026?
11. Is there a spring, cistern or well you would add to the map?

## Projects

12. DECA / UrbanDig (Cisterns Know): does the cistern map data still exist? Can we use it?
13. CARDIMED: where are the restored check dams? Any measurements?
14. Sustainable Cyclades: sources and data behind their water reporting.
15. Andros Routes: spring and mill GIS, oral histories, permissions.
