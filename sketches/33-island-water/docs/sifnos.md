# Sifnos zoom

## Poulati: the commons (ZEPOU24)

- Valley on the east coast; ~15.5 ha irrigated by collectively managed water; cultivated
  plots now under 6% of that (~1 ha).
- 9 springs from sea level to 140 m. Over 30 stone tanks: about 10 small troughs
  (γούρνες, ~3 m³) and 21 open-air cisterns (χαβούζες, 15-20 m³), of which 5 are
  communal and feed secondary cisterns below.
- Furrows (κουτέντο), mostly replaced by plastic pipe; dry stone walls (ξερολιθιές);
  ~21,000 m³ of hand-built stone infrastructure; 34 properties.
- Water is shared by opening outlets for agreed 12-hour spans ("waters") on set weekdays.
  Overflows and drain-offs (στραγγίδια) shared by trust: "no water is ever lost".
- Rights inhere in the land, not the owner. Written into deeds from the early 20th
  century; before that, unwritten.
- Disputes settled by old oral agreement, with stone furrows as evidence. An elder acts
  as informal monitor. Going to court is "unthinkable".
- In droughts, farmed plots are watered first; active farmers quietly use the shares of
  abandoned plots.
- Threats: farming declining (6 farmers in 2022, 4 over 75); land-use change and
  building; the 2015 cadastre separating a communal cistern from its spring; drilling
  into the same aquifer; poorly regulated tourism.

## The island system

- Sources: groundwater, springs, rainwater cisterns, a storage reservoir, desalination
  (ZAF22). Desal: two seawater units at Platys Gialos, one brackish at Kamares (ETHNOS24).
- Residents ~2,755 (2021 census, prelim.); up to 39,000 tourists at peak (PRESSPROJ).
- ~80% of electricity from an oil-fired plant (PRESSPROJ).
- Rooftop cisterns and public springs still in use (TOPOGUIDE); stone check dams being
  revived (CLIMADAPT25).

## The emergency years

See `data/sifnos_timeline.csv`. Key points:

- Jun 2024: desal failure at Platys Gialos, June consumption +100%, two villages without
  water; emergency 20 Jun - 22 Jul 2024. Local reporters found no public notice of it on
  the island (SUSTCYC24).
- 2024: Sifnos in the national seven-island demand study.
- 2026: consultation on a portable 1,000 m³/day RO unit (Jan); €1.05m funding with
  50.84 kWp solar (Jun); emergency water-saving measures (Jul); tender cancelled and
  re-run (Nov); a well-drilling company due (Nov).
