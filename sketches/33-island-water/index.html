A house beside a school roof

A school has a large roof, and stops using water in summer, when the tank would be empty. The school case follows a study of a school on Chios, where it covered about 60% of the school's use; Chios gets far more rain than Sifnos.

How far to trust this. The same model, run on ERA5 rain for Chios, comes within 1.5–6.9 percentage points of all 54 house cases in a published Chios study (Kakoulas et al. 2022), always a little higher, because ERA5 there is about 14% wetter than the stations. ERA5 has not yet been checked against a station on Sifnos.

Sources and limits

  • Daily rain on Sifnos, 1991-01-01 to 2026-10-31: reanalysed. ERA5 (Copernicus Climate Change Service, ECMWF), via the Open-Meteo historical weather API, grid cell 37.00 N, 24.75 E, 3.7 km from Apollonia; downloaded 2026-11-23; CC BY 4.0. Not changed except rounding to 0.1 mm. ERA5 is a weather model fitted to observations on a 0.25° grid (about 25 km); it is not a rain gauge.
  • The adjustment of ERA5 by −12%: calculated from Chios station rain, 2013–2019 (581.7 mm a year), published in Kakoulas, Golfinopoulos, Koumparou & Alexakis, "The Effectiveness of Rainwater Harvesting Infrastructure in a Mediterranean Island", Water 14 (2022) 716, CC BY 4.0, against ERA5 for the same island and years (664 mm). Measured on Chios only.
  • House system costs and the school case: reported, from the same study (Chios prices, 2022).
  • Roof, tank, household size and use: synthetic, set by you. A third of household use for toilets and garden follows the same study.
  • Sifnos desalination capacity, 2,250 m³ a day since 2021: documented, from EMODnet Human Activities (desalination plants, CC BY 4.0, records to 2020), a 2014 review (Zotalis et al., Water 6 (2014) 1134, CC BY 3.0), the municipality's public decisions on Diavgeia and press reports, reconciled plant by plant: 500 and 1,000 m³ a day at Platys Gialos (seawater), 750 at Kamares (brackish).
  • Residents: observed, ELSTAT 2021 census.

What this sketch cannot show: water quality, leaks, evaporation from open cisterns, or how people change their use in a dry year. The model has no first-flush diverter and treats every roof as the same. One ERA5 cell stands for all of Sifnos.
