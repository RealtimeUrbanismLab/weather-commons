Catalog

This working group looks at how weather is monitored, modelled, managed and visualized in real time. We try to understand these practices on their own terms, and to place them in their social, political and global context. We model their effects, not only the weather itself.

Each participant works on their own project. If you already have a research project, bring it. If you are looking for a starting point, begin here.

Categories

Events can come from any of these. Many belong to more than one.

Rainmaking rituals
Ceremonies and practices, in many cultures and over long histories, for calling or asking for rain.
Anti-hail infrastructure
Cannons, rockets and ground generators meant to protect crops from hail; the evidence that they work is mixed.
Cloud seeding
Releasing particles such as silver iodide or salt into clouds to try to increase rain or snow.
Weather militarization
Using weather, or attempts to change it, as a military tool; hostile use was banned by the 1977 ENMOD convention.
Atmospheric simulation
Computer models that calculate how the atmosphere moves and changes, used for forecasts, reanalyses and climate projections.
AI forecasting
Machine-learning models trained on past weather data that produce forecasts, alongside or in place of physics-based models.
Geoengineering
Deliberate, large-scale intervention in the climate system, usually by removing carbon dioxide or reflecting sunlight.
Climate intervention
A term often used in place of geoengineering, sometimes more broadly, for deliberate efforts to change the climate or offset warming.
Earth observation
Satellites, radar, stations, balloons and buoys that measure the atmosphere, ocean and land, and supply the data that models use.

Example sketches

  • 03 Operation Popeye, Laos 1966–1972: the US military's secret cloud seeding over Laos, in four parts, with a week of 1966 wind. A model for adapting to your own event.
  • 01 Realtime Weather pilot: the technical pilot, a 3D Earth with satellite imagery, cloud heights, terrain and a first version of the Popeye case.
  • 02 Haida witness composite: the 2012 iron sulphate dump off Haida Gwaii, an ocean fertilization scheme, as a layered model.

To help you pick

  1. Event. What happened, where and when, and what sources can you find about it?
  2. Simulate and situate. What could you model about it, including its effects? Who decided, who paid, and who lived under it?
  3. Narrate. What one scene would you show someone, and what can your sources not tell you?

Talk it over, post your choice and ask for help in the community space on Ocean Archive.
